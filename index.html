📷 Tips for photographing old prints
  • Take the photo out of its frame or album sleeve to avoid glare.
  • Lay it flat near a window in daylight. Don't use the flash.
  • Hold your phone directly above it so the photo looks square, not slanted.
  • Fill the screen with the photo and tap on the faces to focus.
  • Give your camera lens a quick wipe first. It makes a big difference.

Questions

Good to know

What kind of photos work best?

Any photo where faces are clear works well, in colour or black and white. Old prints are fine: lay them flat by a window and take a photo with your phone.

How will I receive my film?

We email you a link to watch and download it. It plays on any phone, tablet, computer or smart TV, and you can forward it to family.

How long does it take?

Within 5 days of your order, or within 48 hours if you add Priority (+£7.99). For Christmas, order by 15 December and it arrives by 20 December.

Are my photos kept private?

Your photos are only used to make your film. We delete them 30 days after your film is delivered. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do you bring the photos to life?

We use AI tools to repair and gently animate each photo, then a person checks every film and edits it together with your music, names and message before it's sent. Your photos are never used to train AI models, and we delete them 30 days after your film is delivered.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Order the film yourself with their photos, or buy a gift voucher so they can choose their own photos. Voucher codes are emailed within 24 hours and last 12 months.

What if I'm not happy with it?

Tell us and we'll redo it. If it still isn't right, we'll refund you.
