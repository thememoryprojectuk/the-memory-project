Questions

Good to know

What kind of photos work best?

Any photo where faces are clear works well, in colour or black and white. Old prints are fine: lay them flat by a window and take a photo with your phone.

How will I receive my film?

We email you a link to watch and download it. It plays on any phone, tablet, computer or smart TV, and you can forward it to family.

How long does it take?

Within 5 days of your order. For Christmas, order by 11 December and it arrives by 18 December.

Are my photos kept private?

Your photos are only used to make your film. We delete them 30 days after your film is delivered. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do you bring the photos to life?

We use AI tools to repair and gently animate each photo, then a person checks every film and edits it together with your music, names and message before it's sent.

What if I'm not happy with it?

Tell us and we'll redo it. If it still isn't right, we'll refund you.
