2. What we make

We turn the photos you send us into a short digital film. Each photo is repaired and gently animated using AI tools, then set to music with any names, dates and message you choose. A person checks every film before it is sent. Because the animation is created by AI, movements are an artistic interpretation and won't exactly match how the people in the photo moved in real life.
