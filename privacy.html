The Memory Project

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information The Memory Project collects, why, and what we do with it. We keep it simple: we only use your information to make and deliver your film.

Who we are

The Memory Project is a trading name of Luke Moore and Orlando Moore, trading in partnership. Address: 8 Knightcott Road, Bristol BS8 3SB, United Kingdom. We are the "controller" of your personal information. You can contact us about anything in this policy at thememoryprojectteam@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your details: your name and email address.
  • Your order: the film you chose, occasion, date needed, names and message to show on screen, and music choice.
  • Your photos: the photos you send us, which may show you, your family or other people.
  • Payment: payments are handled by our payment provider. We see that you've paid and how much, but we never see or store your full card details.
  • Emails: any messages you send us.
  • Reminder emails: your email address, if you sign up for reminders before Christmas, Mother's Day and Father's Day.
  • Free clip requests: your name, email and the one photo you send us, and whether you asked to hear about offers.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, so there's nothing to accept or turn off. Our website only saves a few small notes in your own browser so it works properly, for example remembering your order reference to show on the thank-you page, a friend's invite link, or that you've already answered "how did you hear about us?". These stay on your device and aren't used to track you.

To see how many people visit our website, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It counts visits without cookies and doesn't identify or follow individual visitors.

Our example film is shown in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube doesn't set cookies until you press play. If you do, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Why we use it

We use your information to take your order, make your film, deliver it to you, take payment and reply to you. Our legal basis is that we need it to carry out our contract with you. We also keep basic order and payment records because tax law requires it.

If you sign up for reminders, or tick the box to hear about offers, we'll occasionally email you about our films and seasonal offers. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any of those emails.

We will never sell your information, use it for marketing without your permission, or share your photos, clip or film publicly unless you clearly agree.

Photos of other people

Family photos often show people other than you, including people who have passed away. When you order, you confirm you have the right to share these photos with us. We only use them to make your film.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run the business. They only process your information on our instructions:

  • Netlify – hosts our website and receives the order form and any photos you upload with it.
  • Google (Gmail and Google Drive) – email, and storing photos and films while we work on them.
  • AI image and video tools – we upload your photos to AI services to repair and animate them. We only use services that do not publish your photos and do not use them to train their AI models. These services may keep a copy for up to 30 days for security checks before deleting it.
  • Our payment provider – to take payment securely.
  • Cloudflare – counts website visits anonymously, without cookies.

Some of these services are based outside the UK, mainly in the United States. Where your information is sent abroad, it is protected by the safeguards UK data protection law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or approved contract terms.

How long we keep it

  • Your photos and finished film: deleted 30 days after your film (or free clip) is delivered.
  • Offer emails list: kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Order and payment records: kept for 6 years, as required for tax purposes.
  • Emails: kept for up to 2 years, then deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to object to how we use it, or to ask us to limit how we use it. Just email us and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
